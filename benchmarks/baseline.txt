# 3D N-Body Simulation – Benchmark Results

## Simulation Configuration
- Particles: 500
- Iterations: 500
- Algorithm: Direct N-Body (O(N²))
- Compiler: GCC 16.2.0
- Optimization: -O3
- Language: C++17
- Parallelization: OpenMP
- Hardware: AMD Ryzen 5 3500U (4 cores / 8 logical threads)

## Median Execution Times

| Configuration | Runtime (seconds) | Speedup |
|---|---:|---:|
| Sequential | 0.906195 | 1.00x |
| OpenMP 1 thread | 0.964595 | 0.94x |
| OpenMP 2 threads | 0.539376 | 1.68x |
| OpenMP 4 threads | 0.486439 | 1.86x |
| OpenMP 8 threads | 0.336241 | 2.70x |

## Parallel Efficiency

| Threads | Efficiency |
|---|---:|
| 1 | 93.95% |

## Correctness Validation
- Initial momentum: (-0.000899685, -0.00113804, 0.00114471)
- Final momentum: (-0.000899685, -0.00113804, 0.00114471)
- Momentum difference: 5.54535e-18

## Preliminary Conclusion
The 8-thread OpenMP implementation achieved the best measured runtime of 0.336241 seconds, corresponding to approximately 2.70x speedup over the sequential implementation.

Further controlled benchmarking is required to reduce the influence of CPU frequency, thermal conditions and background processes.
